Abstraction: AAAI 2024 workshop on cooperative multi-agent decision-making and learning

Key points:

  • Workshop focused on cooperative multi-agent systems (MAS) decision-making and learning from a cognitive modeling perspective
  • Held Feb 26, 2024 (PST) as part of AAAI 2024; invited speakers included Maria Gini, Aaron Courville, Michael Littman, Sven Koenig, and Marco Pavone
  • Key topics include cooperative MARL, multi-agent pathfinding, human-in-the-loop problems, mean field games, and safe AI
  • Contributed papers covered out-of-distribution MARL, cognitive Q-learning, multi-robot navigation, and imperfect-information game training (Mahjong/Bridge)
  • Submissions were non-archival; both short (2-4 page) and long (6-8 page) papers accepted in AAAI 2024 format

Connections: Aaai · Multi Agent Systems · Reinforcement Learning · AI Agents · Cooperative Learning
