To Promote Defense Innovation, Fund the Defense Innovation Unit

defense-techinnovationpentagonstartups

Abstraction: Case for funding Defense Innovation Unit to bridge Pentagon-startup gap

Key points:

  • 2024 DoD Appropriations Bill proposes increasing DIU budget tenfold to >$1B as a "hedge portfolio" of modular systems
  • DIU founded 2015 by Secretary Ash Carter; awarded $1.2B in OTA contracts 2016–2022, generating $4.9B in follow-on production contracts
  • House 2024 NDAA (Section 925) would make DIU director a direct report to the Secretary of Defense and head of the nontraditional innovation fielding enterprise
  • Key problem: "valley of death" between R&D funding and recurring production revenue causes many defense-tech startups to fail
  • Ukraine war demonstrated value of both major-contractor exquisite systems and startup-built lower-cost technologies (drones, ISR)
  • Shield AI and Anduril hold DIU contracts; most successful defense startups (Palantir, Anduril) were founded by already-wealthy entrepreneurs
