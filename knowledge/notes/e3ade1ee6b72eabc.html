Abstraction: Inscribe raises $25M Series B for AI-powered financial document fraud detection

Key points:

  • Founded by Conor and Ronan Burke after Conor experienced manual document-review fraud blind spots at an Irish bank; platform trained on hundreds of millions of data points
  • Parses, classifies, and data-matches financial onboarding documents (bank statements, pay stubs, IDs); highlights discrepancies and generates individual customer risk profiles in seconds
  • Key differentiation: proprietary dataset built through customer partnerships from day one, solving the "cold start problem" that killed competitors who built models before getting data
  • Core philosophy: "automation without fraud detection is reckless"—credit analysis and fraud detection must be bundled, not separated
  • Average US fintech loses $51M/year to fraud; Inscribe clients include TripActions, Ramp, Bluevine, and Shift
  • $25M Series B led by Threshold Ventures brings total raised to $38M; company plans to double from 50 to ~100 employees within 18 months
