Silicon Valley Bank Review – Supervisory Materials

svbbank-supervisionfederal-reservebanking-regulation

Abstraction: Federal Reserve public release of SVB supervisory documents post-failure

Key points:

  • The Federal Reserve released confidential supervisory documents about SVB and SVB Financial Group (SVBFG) citing extraordinary public interest outweighing confidentiality
  • Documents span 2016–2023 and include supervisory letters on corporate governance, liquidity, capital planning, risk management, and internal audit
  • Key warning letters include a 2021 liquidity planning letter and a 2022 governance and risk management letter — both issued before the March 2023 collapse
  • CAMELS examination reports (Capital, Asset Quality, Management, Earnings, Liquidity, Sensitivity) released from 2016 through 2022
  • A Feb 2023 board briefing on rising interest rates and supervisory approach shows regulators were tracking the risk shortly before failure
  • A draft Memorandum of Understanding dated March 10, 2023 — the day of SVB's closure — was also released
