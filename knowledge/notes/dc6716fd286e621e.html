Facebook's Quest to Build an Artificial Brain Depends on This Guy

deep-learningconvolutional-neural-networksyann-lecunfacebookai-history

Abstraction: Yann LeCun pioneers convolutional neural networks and leads Facebook AI lab

Key points:

  • Zuckerberg recruited LeCun to run Facebook AI Research (FAIR); IEEE gave LeCun Neural Network Pioneer Award
  • LeNet (Bell Labs, 1993) was the first practical convolutional net: read handwritten digits on bank checks
  • Convolutional nets reuse spatial filters across image locations, making them scalable and memory-efficient
  • Stochastic gradient descent emerged as a "hack" due to weak French hardware; calculates error per example rather than batch average
  • Backpropagation formalized by Hinton/Rumelhart/Williams in 1986 Nature paper; LeCun independently developed similar approach
  • Companies deploying convnets in 2014: Google (Android voice), Baidu (visual search), Microsoft, Twitter
