$450 and 19 hours is all it takes to rival OpenAI's o1-preview

open-source-aireasoning-modelsfine-tuninglow-cost-trainingsky-t1

Abstraction: UC Berkeley Sky-T1-32B open-source reasoning model built for $450 in 19 hours

Key points:

  • NovaSky team at UC Berkeley released Sky-T1-32B-Preview: fine-tuned from Alibaba's Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct using synthetic data from QwQ-32B-Preview
  • Total training cost $450 (Lambda Cloud pricing) on 8 Nvidia H100 GPUs in 19 hours
  • Matches or exceeds OpenAI's o1-preview on math and coding benchmarks; does not surpass o1 on GPQA-Diamond graduate-level science questions
  • Full open-source release: weights, data, infrastructure, and technical details
  • Demonstrates that high-level reasoning capabilities can be replicated affordably using synthetic training data and open models
  • Contrast: GPT-4 suspected to cost ~$78M in compute; Sky-T1 shows academic labs can compete at a fraction of the cost
