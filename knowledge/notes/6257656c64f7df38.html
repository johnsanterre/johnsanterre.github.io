Abstraction: CB Insights annual list of 100 top private AI startups globally for 2022

Key points:

  • 2022 cohort raised $12B+ across 300+ equity deals since 2017; includes 16 unicorns valued at $1B+
  • Roughly one-third of winners focus on AI development tools (data annotation, model training, bias monitoring); 43 companies target industry-specific applications
  • Healthcare leads industry categories with 10 companies; finance and insurance second with 7
  • 73 of 100 winners are US-headquartered; UK second (8), Canada third (5)
  • Notable entrants: Agility Robotics (humanoid warehouse robots), Canvas Construction (AI drywall robots, Boston Dynamics alumni), GGWP (anti-toxicity in gaming)
  • 2021 cohort saw $6B in follow-on funding, 9 exits including acquisitions by Meta and Nvidia, and 6 new unicorns
