UC Berkeley is partnering with Coursera to train graduate students in engineering online. Here's what you need to know | Fortune Education

online-educationgraduate-engineeringprofessional-developmentedtech

Abstraction: UC Berkeley online MAS-E engineering degree for working professionals

Key points:

  • Berkeley's College of Engineering launches a Master of Advanced Study in Engineering (MAS-E) fully online through Coursera, starting summer 2024
  • Program costs approximately $42,000; 15% of tuition revenue is reserved for student financial aid
  • Students complete 22 one-credit asynchronous micro-courses across five themes plus a two-credit capstone; expected completion in 9–24 months
  • Five themes: infrastructure systems, biomedical/biomechanical, engineering data analysis, advanced manufacturing/materials, and electrical/power/autonomous systems
  • Initial cohort targets 100–200 students, with planned growth to ~500; applications open September 2023
  • Engineering master's graduates (class of 2023) had the highest projected starting salary of any discipline at $86,826
