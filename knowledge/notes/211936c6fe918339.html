The Benefits of an "Everything" Notebook in NotebookLM - KDnuggets

notebooklmknowledge-managementsecond-brainsemantic-searchdata-science

Abstraction: Centralized NotebookLM notebook as a queryable professional knowledge base

Key points:

  • NotebookLM supports up to 50 sources per notebook with up to 25 million words total; each source can be up to 500,000 words
  • Capacity hack: consolidate many small documents into 50 master Google Docs organized by domain to maximize the word limit
  • Key advantage over traditional search: cross-document reasoning — NotebookLM synthesizes across sources rather than returning a list of links
  • Semantic retrieval replaces keyword search: describe a concept you vaguely remember and NotebookLM finds it with citations
  • "Everything" notebook becomes a personalized tutor: can generate quizzes, flashcards, explain complex topics, draft summaries from ingested material
  • Treat the notebook as a living document: ingest project reports, post-mortems, code snippets, and meeting transcripts continuously
