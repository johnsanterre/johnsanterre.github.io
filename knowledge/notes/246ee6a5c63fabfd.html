AWS Educate - Cloud Skills for Education- AWS

cloud-computingeducationawslearning-platform

Abstraction: Free AWS cloud and AI skills learning platform for all ages

Key points:

  • AWS Educate is open to any individual regardless of education level or experience; no credit card required
  • Learners as young as 13 can register with just an email address and access free hands-on labs
  • Offers self-paced video and lab-based training across a range of cloud and AI topics
  • Learners earn shareable digital badges to showcase completed skills
  • Learners 18+ can access the AWS Educate Job Board to search in-demand jobs and internships worldwide

Connections: Aws · Cloud Computing
