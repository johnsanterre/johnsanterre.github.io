How Many Images Does It Take? Estimating Imitation Thresholds in Text-to-Image Models

text-to-imagecopyrightimitationgenerative-aitraining-data

Abstraction: Empirical threshold at which text-to-image models begin imitating training concepts

Key points:

  • Imitation threshold: the minimum training instances of a concept needed for a model to generate recognizably similar output — estimated at 200–700 images depending on domain and model
  • Studied two domains: human faces and art styles; evaluated four text-to-image models on three pretraining datasets
  • Proposes an efficient estimation approach that avoids training models from scratch for each test
  • Provides an empirical basis for copyright violation claims and a guiding principle for model developers aiming to comply with privacy and copyright law
  • Code available at github.com/vsahil/MIMETIC-2
