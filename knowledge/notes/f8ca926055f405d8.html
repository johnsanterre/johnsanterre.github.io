AMP 97.1 FM making a 'Morning Mess' by airing LA version of Arizona show

Abstraction: LA radio station AMP 97.1 FM launches localized morning show

Key points:

  • AMP 97.1 FM (Entercom) launched the LA edition of "The Morning Mess" in August 2020 with hosts Joey Boy, Aneesh Ratan, Jeana Shepard, and Karla Hernandez
  • The show is an adaptation of the Phoenix-based Live 101.5 FM program with Southern California-specific content
  • Entercom simultaneously restructured KROQ 106.7 FM, replacing the Kevin & Bean legacy show with Stryker & Klein
