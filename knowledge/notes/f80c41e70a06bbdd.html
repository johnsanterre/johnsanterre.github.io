Finding Common Ground on AI — Semafor Event

ai-policyai-governancewashington-dcbicoastalregulationevent

Abstraction: Semafor DC event bridging Silicon Valley and Washington AI perspectives

Key points:

  • Event held December 7, 2023 at the Gallup Building, Washington DC
  • Premise: Silicon Valley views AI with optimism/existential fear; Washington sees it as Big Tech disruption requiring regulation
  • Speakers included Google's Kent Walker, FDA Chief Scientist Namandjé Bumpus, Perplexity AI CEO Aravind Srinivas, Future of Life Institute's Anthony Aguirre, and several US Representatives
  • Topics spanned AI limitations, work/employment, disease, warfare, democratic elections, and AI's fundamental nature
  • Defense Innovation Unit, FDA, and DOE all represented, reflecting government-wide AI engagement
  • Scale AI and Cisco among private sector participants

Connections: Google · Perplexity AI · AI Governance · AI Policy
