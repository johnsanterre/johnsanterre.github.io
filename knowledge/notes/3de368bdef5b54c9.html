Scientists' Side Hustle? Using AI and Quantum Computing to Generate New Peptides

quantum-computinggenerative-aidrug-discoverypeptides

Abstraction: DTU hybrid quantum-classical generative model for peptide binders

Key points:

  • Technical University of Denmark team (Timothy Patrick Jenkins) paired a generative protein model with a small Orca Computing photonic quantum computer.
  • Lab-made peptides from the hybrid model bound their targets more often than the classical model's, with the biggest gains where training data was scarce.
  • Motivation: genetic data skews to Western populations, so models need diversity for understudied groups in Asia and Africa.
  • Funded on weekends with leftover project money because 'most innovative science is too scary for foundations'.
