Tech companies like Google and Meta made cuts to DEI programs in 2023 after big promises in prior years

deidiversity-equity-inclusiontech-industrygooglemetaai-bias

Abstraction: Big tech DEI program rollback after 2020 George Floyd commitments

Key points:

  • DEI-related job postings declined 44% by mid-2023 year-over-year, reversing a ~30% expansion from 2020–2021
  • Google cancelled its 2023 Early Career Immersion (ECI) cohort and cut DEI leadership; Meta laid off its entire Sourcer Development Program (~60 workers)
  • Some DEI consulting clients had their budgets cut by as much as 90% by mid-2023
  • Experts warn reduced DEI representation in AI development teams will embed historical biases into AI products, widening existing power imbalances
  • Supreme Court's June 2023 ruling ending affirmative action in college admissions and political "anti-woke" backlash contributed to the corporate retreat
  • Third-party DEI organizations (AfroTech/Blavity, Grace Hopper/AnitaB.org) lost corporate sponsorships, threatening the broader ecosystem
