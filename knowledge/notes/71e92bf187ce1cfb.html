Abstraction: Vantor geospatial intelligence platform unifying satellite and sensor data

Key points:

  • Vantor provides a spatial intelligence platform anchoring all sensor data (space, air, ground) to an AI-ready geospatial ground truth for defense, intelligence, and commercial sectors
  • Powers 90% of foundational geospatial intelligence used by the US Government; serves 60+ government partners worldwide
  • WorldView product line offers 2D satellite imagery, radar (SAR via Umbra), and orbital intelligence; resolution down to 15 cm for most populous areas and 30 cm for 99% of the world
  • Satellite constellation enables 15× daily collection opportunities per location; ~7 million sq km daily high-resolution collection capacity
  • Over 1 billion people rely on navigation apps powered by Vantor's dynamic foundation; 100 million+ sq km mapped in 3D
  • Key capabilities: automated intelligence cycle (tasking → production → fusion → AI analysis) and multi-sensor fusion via accurate spatial foundation
