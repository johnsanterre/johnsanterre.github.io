I tried using NotebookLM's new AI Video Overviews and ended up with some usefully informative, but rather dull PowerPoint presentations

notebooklmai-content-generationvideo-overviewgoogle

Abstraction: NotebookLM Video Overviews feature review — informative but visually weak

Key points:

  • NotebookLM added Video Overviews: ~7-minute narrated slideshows generated from uploaded sources, joining existing Audio Overviews
  • Single AI narrator voice with text and illustrations on slides; easy to generate from source documents
  • Tested on Hieronymus Bosch and the TV show Taskmaster — content was accurate but lacked relevant imagery (no actual artwork or screenshots)
  • Customizable via prompts specifying goals, audience, and tone; future format options teased by Google
  • Best suited as a private study tool or educational onboarding aid rather than shareable content
  • Described as "AI equivalent of dry toast" — functional but not engaging

Connections: Google · Notebooklm · AI Content Generation · Multimodal AI
