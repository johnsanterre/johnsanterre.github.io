Abstraction: Curated list of freely available data visualization learning resources

Key points:

  • 17-page PDF by Jock D. Mackinlay (Tableau) covers history from cave drawings to dashboards and human perception tricks
  • 25-page PDF by HubSpot & Visage covers finding data stories, 3 pattern-finding methods, and 7 chart types with dos and don'ts
  • 600-page free D3.js book by Malcolm Maclean includes 50+ downloadable code examples from basics to mapping
  • 160-page PDF by Tactical Technology Collective focuses on visual persuasion and getting audiences to care about data
  • 13-page Sunlight Foundation guide covers layout, color, font recommendations and chart type selection
  • Multiple short PDFs (13-16 pages) from Intel, FusionCharts, and Joel Laumans cover beginner fundamentals
