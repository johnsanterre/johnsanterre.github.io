Abstraction: AI accelerating Pentagon kill chain planning amid usage policy tensions

Key points:

  • Pentagon CDAO Dr. Radha Plumb confirmed AI gives "significant advantage" in identifying, tracking, and assessing threats and speeds "execution of kill chain" in planning/strategy phases
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta all revised usage policies in 2024 to allow US defense/intelligence use while still prohibiting AI from directly harming humans
  • Key partnerships: Meta+Lockheed/Booz Allen, Anthropic+Palantir, OpenAI+Anduril, Cohere+Palantir
  • Pentagon maintains human-in-the-loop requirement for all lethal force decisions as matter of "reliability and ethics"
  • Anthropic's Evan Hubinger argues engaging with the US government on AI safety is necessary, not avoidable
  • Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly defended military AI work as a responsible middle ground between full prohibition and unrestricted use
