Full Committee Hearing - Artificial Intelligence Advancing Innovation Towards the National Interest

ai-policycongressai-regulationnational-securityinnovation

Abstraction: US House Science Committee 2023 hearing on AI policy and national interest

Key points:

  • Full committee hearing held June 22, 2023 at 2318 Rayburn House Office Building; chaired by Frank Lucas
  • Witnesses: Dr. Jason Matheny (RAND Corporation President/CEO), Dr. Shahin Farshchi (Lux Capital General Partner), Clement Delangue (HuggingFace Co-founder/CEO), Dr. Rumman Chowdhury (Harvard Responsible AI Fellow), Dr. Dewey Murdick (Center for Security and Emerging Technology)
  • Covered AI innovation, national security implications, responsible AI, and emerging technology policy
  • Hugging Face represented the open-source AI perspective in testimony
  • RAND and CSET witnesses focused on national interest and security dimensions

Connections: Hugging Face · Rand Corporation · AI Policy · AI Regulation · AI Safety
