Scientists have a 20-minute "conversation" with a humpback whale named Twain

whale-communicationsetinon-human-intelligencebioacousticsanimal-language

Abstraction: Whale-SETI project achieves first humpback whale communicative exchange

Key points:

  • Whale-SETI team (SETI Institute, UC Davis, Alaska Whale Foundation) played a recorded humpback "contact call" underwater; whale Twain approached and mirrored interval variations for 20 minutes
  • Authors claim this is the first communicative exchange between humans and humpback whales conducted in the humpback's own "language"
  • The research uses information theory mathematics to quantify communicative complexity as a method also applicable to potential extraterrestrial signals
  • Humpback whales exhibit complex social behaviors including bubble-net cooperative feeding and distinct vocal dialects across populations
  • AI algorithms trained on whale calls and human language are used to detect language-like patterns in whale vocalizations
  • SETI assumption that extraterrestrials will initiate contact is supported by the whale's proactive approach behavior
