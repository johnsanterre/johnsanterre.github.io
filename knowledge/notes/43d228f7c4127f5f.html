New Data Shows Just How Badly OpenAI And Perplexity Are Screwing Over Publishers

ai-searchweb-scrapingpublishersreferral-trafficcopyrightperplexity

Abstraction: AI search engines send 96% less referral traffic while massively increasing scraping

Key points:

  • TollBit report (analyzing 160 websites, Q4 2024): AI search engines send 96% less referral traffic to news sites and blogs than traditional Google search; AI scrapers visited sites 2 million times on average per site.
  • AI scraping has more than doubled in recent months; each page scraped ~7 times on average; companies like OpenAI and Perplexity often use unidentified bots making it hard for publishers to block.
  • Chegg's traffic plummeted 49% YoY in January after Google released AI summaries; Chegg sued Google and is considering going private.
  • Perplexity scraped one publisher 500 times after being blocked yet sent 10,000+ referrals — evidence of covert scraping under a different user agent despite claiming robots.txt compliance.
  • Content licensing deals (AP, Axel Springer, Financial Times with OpenAI) and new platforms like TollBit (500 publishers, charges per scrape) are emerging as partial solutions.
  • Publishers face a dilemma with Google: blocking its AI bots risks harming SEO since the same bots may serve multiple purposes (indexing + AI training).
