Algorithms Illuminated Omnibus Edition (September 2022)

algorithmstextbookcomputer-scienceeducation

Abstraction: Tim Roughgarden's self-published algorithms textbook series omnibus edition

Key points:

  • 690-page hardcover omnibus collecting all four parts of the "Algorithms Illuminated" series; list price $60; distributed by Cambridge University Press
  • Originally a DIY self-published series (4 individual parts available as paperback/ebook via Amazon and regional distributors)
  • Translations available in Chinese (Posts & Telecom Press), Korean (Insight Publishing), Russian (Piter Publishing), Spanish (OJ Books)
  • Companion videos and resources available on the website; author Tim Roughgarden also active on Twitter (@Tim_Roughgarden)
  • Examination copies available to instructors via the publisher; aimed at undergraduate algorithms courses
