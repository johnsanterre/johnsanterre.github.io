Abstraction: Using Gemini and NotebookLM for structured self-guided career learning

Key points:

  • Four-step framework: assess learning needs, design a custom plan, personalize a study guide, and study interactively
  • Gemini Deep Research can produce thorough industry analysis; prompts should include role and context for relevance
  • NotebookLM supports uploading PDFs, links, audio/video, and notes; Mind Maps feature visualizes learning content
  • Gemini Gems (customizable AI assistants) such as Learning Coach provide step-by-step guided learning activities
  • Gemini Canvas Quizzes test knowledge and flag areas for improvement with adaptive follow-up
  • NotebookLM Audio Overviews can generate personal podcasts from study materials for on-the-go learning
