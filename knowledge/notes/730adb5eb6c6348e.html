Abstraction: Fundamental inequality bounding inner product by product of vector norms

Key points:

  • States |<u,v>| <= ||u|| * ||v|| for all vectors in an inner product space; equality iff u and v are linearly dependent
  • Published for sums by Cauchy (1821), for integrals by Bunyakovsky (1859) and Schwarz (1888) independently
  • Implies triangle inequality, continuity of inner product, and enables definition of angles in real inner product spaces
  • Sedrakyan's/Engel's/Titu's lemma is a direct consequence: sum(a_i^2/b_i) >= (sum a_i)^2 / sum(b_i)
  • Used to prove spectral theorem for self-adjoint operators in finite dimensions; also proves Mantel's theorem in extremal graph theory (triangle-free graphs have at most n^2/4 edges)
  • Generalizes to Holder's inequality for Lp norms and extends to C*-algebras (Kadison-Schwarz inequality for positive maps)
