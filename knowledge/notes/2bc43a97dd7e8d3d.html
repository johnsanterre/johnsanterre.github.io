Abstraction: Results of the 2021 NIST ARIAC simulated robotics manufacturing competition

Key points:

  • ARIAC (Agile Robotics for Industrial Automation Competition) is a yearly NIST competition using ROS and Gazebo to simulate a factory floor with logistics and assembly tasks.
  • 2021 was the fifth annual competition; participants scored on speed, quality, and a panel of three judges across multiple trials.
  • First place: Team Reaper (Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences); 2nd: Team TheItalianJob (Ekumen); 3rd: Team Sirius ($10,000 prize); 4th: Team Boston University ECE ($5,000); 5th: Team UTA Robotic Vision Lab ($2,500).
  • Participants ranged from university research groups to small businesses to a high school robotics club (Dublin Robotics Club, 6th place).
  • Manufacturing simulations ("digital twins") let engineers evaluate new approaches in software before committing to costly physical infrastructure.
