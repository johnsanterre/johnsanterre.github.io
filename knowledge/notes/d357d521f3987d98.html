AI is already linked to layoffs in the industry that created it | CNN Business

Abstraction: AI driving tech sector layoffs and workforce skill reshuffling

Key points:

  • Chegg cut 4% of staff (~80 people) citing AI strategy realignment in mid-2023
  • Dropbox cut 16% (~500 people) while simultaneously hiring for "New AI Initiatives"
  • Challenger, Gray & Christmas reported 3,900 AI-attributed layoffs in May 2023, all in tech
  • AI/ML senior software engineers earn 12% more than peers and saw 4% salary growth YTD vs. flat for the broader group
  • Dropbox listed Principal ML Engineer roles at $276,300–$373,800 base vs. $171,895 average for senior SWEs
  • Columbia Business School professor Dan Wang frames it as "human specialists replaced by human specialists who can use AI tools"
