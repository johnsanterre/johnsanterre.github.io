Abstraction: GMF tracker mapping US federal AI policy actions and initiatives since 2020

Key points:

  • GMF Digital's tracker catalogs US federal AI actions: executive branch, agencies, and Congress
  • Coverage begins with first executive branch actions in 2020, listed in reverse chronological order
  • Tracks goals, applications, enforcement mechanisms, timing, and legal outcomes of each initiative
  • Highlights variation in agency approaches and differing degrees of enforceability
  • Some actions apply existing laws to AI rather than creating new AI-specific law
  • EU-level action tracker planned as a complement to the US tracker
