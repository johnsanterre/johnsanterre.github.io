Formalization of QFT

formal-verificationlean4quantum-field-theoryinteractive-theorem-provingai-assisted-formalization

Abstraction: Lean 4 machine-checked proof of free bosonic quantum field theory axioms

Key points:

  • Formalizes the construction of free bosonic QFT in 4D Euclidean spacetime and its proof of Glimm-Jaffe (Osterwalder-Schrader) axioms using Lean 4 interactive theorem prover
  • Initially assumed three auxiliary results (Minlos' theorem, nuclear property of Schwartz space, Goursat's theorem); all subsequently proved by the group and Lean community contributors
  • AI coding assistants were used throughout the development process
  • Proof of concept that extended arguments in mathematical physics can be translated into machine-checked proofs with existing AI tools
  • Discusses how AI-assisted formalization may influence the future of theoretical physics research
  • Michael R. Douglas et al. (2026); uses Lean's Mathlib library
