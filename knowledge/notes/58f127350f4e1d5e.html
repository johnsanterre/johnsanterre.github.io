IBM, HuggingFace, and NASA Open-Sources Watsonx.ai Foundation Model

geospatial-aifoundation-modelsopen-sourceclimate-scienceremote-sensing

Abstraction: Open-source geospatial foundation model for climate and Earth research

Key points:

  • IBM and Hugging Face released NASA's first open geospatial foundation model, the largest geospatial model on HuggingFace
  • Trained on Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 (HLS) satellite data across the continental US for one year
  • Shows 15% improvement over existing techniques while requiring only half the labeled data
  • Can be fine-tuned for deforestation tracking, crop yield prediction, and greenhouse gas monitoring
  • A 250,000-terabyte influx of new mission data anticipated by 2024 creates urgent need for such models
  • Commercial version integrated into IBM Environmental Intelligence Suite (EIS) planned for later in 2023
