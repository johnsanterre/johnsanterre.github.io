Abstraction: Daily LeetCode practice builds professional software engineering skills beyond interviews

Key points:

  • Author (front-end engineer) completed 400+ LeetCode problems over one year as a non-interview professional development habit
  • Key benefits: automatic edge-case thinking, ~50% faster problem-solving speed after 6 months, reduced fear of complex tasks
  • Mastering one topic deeply before moving on (easy → medium → hard within a domain) accelerates pattern recognition
  • Posting solutions and writing clear explanations transfers to workplace communication and code quality
  • Daily 30-minute consistent habit more effective than sporadic intensive sessions; tracking streaks builds accountability
  • JavaScript-specific: regular practice deepens language fluency (e.g., knowing exactly what .slice() vs .splice() does)
