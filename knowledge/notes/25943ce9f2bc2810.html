Google's medical AI chatbot is already being tested in hospitals

medical-aillmgooglehealthcarepalm-2

Abstraction: Google Med-PaLM 2 hospital pilot tests medical LLM accuracy and safety

Key points:

  • Google's Med-PaLM 2 has been in testing at Mayo Clinic and other hospitals since April 2023, announced at Google I/O in May
  • Trained on curated medical expert demonstrations to outperform general chatbots (Bard, Bing, ChatGPT) on healthcare conversations
  • Physicians found more inaccuracies and irrelevant information in Med-PaLM 2 answers vs. actual doctor answers, per a May 2023 Google study
  • In nearly all other metrics (reasoning, consensus-supported answers, comprehension), Med-PaLM 2 performed comparably to doctors
  • Customer data is encrypted and Google does not have access to it during testing
  • Google senior research director Greg Corrado said he wouldn't want it for his own family's healthcare but believes it expands beneficial AI healthcare applications "by 10-fold"

Connections: Google · Mayo Clinic · Large Language Models · Medical AI · AI Safety
