DARPA Advances Quantum Computing Initiative

quantum-computingdarpagovernment-researchbenchmarking

Abstraction: DARPA Quantum Benchmarking Initiative advances 11 companies to Stage B validation

Key points:

  • 11 companies advanced to Stage B of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative (QBI), covering the most diverse qubit technologies in a single DARPA program
  • Stage B involves a year-long deep dive into R&D plans, prototype roadmaps, and risk-mitigation; Stage C will be independent hardware verification
  • Goal is to determine if an industrially useful quantum computer (computational value exceeding cost) can be built by 2033
  • Companies span five qubit modalities: trapped ion (IonQ, Quantinuum), neutral atom (QuEra, Atom Computing), superconducting (IBM, Nord Quantique), silicon spin (Diraq, Photonic Inc., Quantum Motion, Silicon Quantum Computing), and photonic (Xanadu)
  • DARPA frames QBI not as a single-winner competition but as merit-based evaluation of each approach independently
