UChicago launches landmark initiative with IonQ to advance quantum research and collaboration

quantum-computingquantum-networkingacademia-industry-partnershiptrapped-ion

Abstraction: UChicago and IonQ partnership advancing quantum computing and networking

Key points:

  • IonQ will become the third core partner of the Chicago Quantum Exchange (CQE), joining Boeing and IBM
  • The new IonQ Center for Engineering and Science will house UChicago PME and CQE offices; Illinois committed $175M in 2019 for quantum infrastructure
  • The fiber quantum testbed connecting UChicago, Argonne, and Fermilab will expand; at 124 miles it is already one of the nation's longest quantum networks
  • IonQ's production-grade trapped-ion quantum computers and entanglement-distribution network will be deployed directly on a university campus for the first time
  • Nature ranked UChicago first among U.S. universities in quantum research in 2025
  • Initiative could seed more than 140 research and technology development projects
