The Missing Semester of Your CS Education

cs-educationdeveloper-toolsshellgitsoftware-engineering

Abstraction: MIT IAP course covering practical developer tools CS curricula omit

Key points:

  • Free MIT course (co-taught by Anish Athalye, Jon Gjengset, Jose Javier Gonzalez) covering shell, command-line environments, debugging, profiling, version control, and code quality
  • 2026 syllabus added an "Agentic Coding" lecture integrating AI-enabled tools across all lectures rather than a standalone AI topic
  • Freely available on YouTube with translations into 17+ languages; featured on Hacker News in 2019, 2020, and 2026
  • Premise: students spend hundreds of hours with developer tools yet rarely receive formal instruction on them
  • Previous year topics include data wrangling, security/cryptography, backups, and OS customization

Connections: Mit · Cs Education · Developer Tools · Agentic Coding · Version Control
