Abstraction: Google Gemini LearnLM integration across education products at I/O 2025

Key points:

  • LearnLM capabilities infused directly into Gemini 2.5 (announced at I/O 2025); Gemini 2.5 Pro outperformed all competitors on every learning science principles category per Google's evaluation
  • NotebookLM gains Audio Overviews length control (quick vs. deep) and upcoming Video Overviews; Illuminate experiment (Google Labs) offers transcripts and a public library of research paper discussions
  • Google Search AI Mode gains Gemini 2.5 integration, Deep Search (deep research), and Search Live (Project Astra real-time visual Q&A, coming to Labs in summer 2025)
  • Gemini app adds interactive quiz creation globally for students 18+; free Gemini upgrade for college students expanded to Brazil, Indonesia, Japan, and UK (sign up by June 30, 2025)
  • New Labs experiments: Sparkify (text/ideas to short animated video via Gemini + Veo); Project Astra conversational tutor prototype with step-by-step homework help and diagram generation

Connections: Google · Gemini · Notebooklm · Learnlm · Educational AI · Multimodal AI
