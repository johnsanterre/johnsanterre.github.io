Abstraction: Free graduate math course notes covering algebra through arithmetic geometry

Key points:

  • Over 2,022 pages of graduate-level course notes taught since 1986, covering Group Theory, Fields and Galois Theory, Algebraic Geometry, Algebraic Number Theory, Class Field Theory, and more
  • Notes span first through third year graduate level; table of prerequisites provided for each course
  • Available as PDF (standard), cropped PDF (eReader), and regular PDF; some available as published books
  • Algebraic Geometry notes study abstract algebraic varieties (not just subvarieties of affine/projective space), leading naturally into scheme theory
  • Includes advanced topics: Abelian Varieties, Étale Cohomology, Complex Multiplication, Lie Algebras and Algebraic Groups
