Coding Interviews were HARD, until I learned these Patterns

coding-interviewsalgorithmsdata-structuresleetcode

Abstraction: 15 essential algorithmic patterns for solving coding interview problems

Key points:

  • 15 core patterns cover the vast majority of LeetCode/FAANG interview problems: Two Pointers, Prefix Sum, Sliding Window, Fast & Slow Pointers (Floyd's cycle detection), In-place Linked List Reversal, Monotonic Stack, Top K Elements (heaps), Overlapping Intervals, Modified Binary Search, Tree Traversals, DFS, BFS, Matrix Traversal, Backtracking, and Dynamic Programming
  • Two Pointers: avoids nested loops for pair/sum problems on sorted arrays or linked lists
  • Sliding Window: maintains a variable-size contiguous subarray window for substring/subarray problems
  • Fast & Slow Pointers (tortoise & hare): detects cycles in linked lists and finds middle elements
  • Dynamic Programming: breaks problems into optimal subproblems; covers classics like Climbing Stairs (70) and Longest Increasing Subsequence (300)
  • Recommended resources: Grokking the Coding Interview (DesignGurus), Algomonster, Educative-99 (99 curated problems vs. 2800 LeetCode)
