Meta unveils HOT3D dataset for advanced computer vision training

datasetcomputer-visionhand-trackingegocentric-videoroboticsar-vr

Abstraction: Egocentric 3D hand-object interaction dataset from Meta Reality Labs

Key points:

  • HOT3D contains 833+ minutes (3.7M+ images) of egocentric multi-view RGB and monochrome video of hand-object interactions
  • Dataset covers 19 subjects interacting with 33 diverse rigid objects in office and household scenarios
  • Collected using Meta's Project Aria prototype AR glasses and commercially available Quest 3 VR headset
  • Ground-truth 3D poses obtained via professional motion-capture with optical markers; hand annotations in UmeTrack and MANO formats
  • Multi-view models trained on HOT3D significantly outperform single-view counterparts on 3D hand tracking, 6DoF object pose estimation, and 3D lifting
  • Dataset is publicly available and targets robotics, AR/VR, and human-machine interface research
