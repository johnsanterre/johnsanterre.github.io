Teaching with AI

educationchatgptpromptingai-tools

Abstraction: OpenAI guide for educators using ChatGPT in classrooms

Key points:

  • OpenAI released a teacher-facing guide with prompt templates, FAQ, and caveats about limitations and bias
  • Use cases: debate partner persona, quiz/lesson plan drafting, translation/writing aid for non-native English speakers, critical thinking practice
  • Ethan and Lilach Mollick (Wharton Interactive) contributed multi-turn instructional prompts for lesson planning, concept explanation, and Socratic tutoring
  • Teachers advised that ChatGPT outputs are a starting point — outputs may be inaccurate and should be verified
  • Goal is to strengthen students' original critical thinking and creativity, not replace them
  • FAQ covers how ChatGPT works, AI detector efficacy, and bias

Connections: Openai · Chatgpt · AI In Education · Prompt Engineering
