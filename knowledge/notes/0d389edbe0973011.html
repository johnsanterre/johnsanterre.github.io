Abstraction: Open educational resource covering probability, statistics, and stochastic processes

Key points:

  • Comprehensive educational website by Kyle Siegrist integrating expository text, interactive web apps, datasets, and biographical sketches
  • Covers probability, mathematical statistics, and stochastic processes for teachers and students
  • Partially funded by two NSF Course and Curriculum Development Program grants (awards DUE-9652870 and DUE-0089377)
  • Uses HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, and MathJax for mathematical notation rendering
  • Licensed under Creative Commons (free to copy, distribute, modify, and commercialize with attribution)
  • Affiliated with the University of Alabama in Huntsville

Connections: National Science Foundation · Probability Theory · Mathematical Statistics · Stochastic Processes
