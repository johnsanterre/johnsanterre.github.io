Billionaires including Eric Schmidt plow $300 million into a non-profit that is France's latest push to catch up in AI | Fortune

franceai-labsovereign-aiopen-sourcenonprofit

Abstraction: France's €300M open-source AI research nonprofit kyutai launched

Key points:

  • Xavier Niel (Iliad) and Rodolphe Saadé (CMA CGM) each invest €100M; Eric Schmidt contributes an undisclosed amount via Schmidt Ventures
  • The lab, called kyutai, will produce open-source research and is headquartered in Paris
  • Scientists recruited include former Google DeepMind researchers Neil Zeghidour and Laurent Mazare, and Meta alumni Hervé Jégou, Edouard Grave, and Alexandre Defossez
  • Niel and Saadé are also seed investors in Mistral AI, France's leading generative AI startup
  • The initiative reflects European concern about US tech giants (Microsoft, Google, Amazon) dwarfing continental AI investment

Connections: Eric Schmidt · Kyutai · Mistral AI · Google Deepmind · Sovereign AI · Open Source AI
