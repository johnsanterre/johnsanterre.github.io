CV4Animals Workshop

computer-visionanimalsbehavior-analysistrackingworkshopcvpr

Abstraction: CVPR workshop applying computer vision to animal detection tracking and behavior understanding

Key points:

  • CV4Animals brings together researchers from computer vision, neuroscience, biology, ecology, and conservation
  • Goal: scalable objective repeatable measurement of animal behaviors beyond manual expert annotation
  • 2026 edition at CVPR; previous editions in 2023, 2024, 2025; outstanding papers invited to IJCV special issue
  • Community of 2500+ researchers via the "AI for Conservation" Slack (Sara Beery)
  • Organizers include experts from Tsinghua University, UC San Diego (comparative social cognition), and CU Boulder (firefly/honeybee collective behavior)
  • Dual submission tracks: unpublished 4-page CVPR-format papers (double-blind) and already-published peer-reviewed work

Connections: Cvpr · Computer Vision · Animal Behavior Analysis · Object Tracking
