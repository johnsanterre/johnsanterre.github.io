Which one result in mathematics has surprised you the most?

mathematicssurprising-resultsset-theorycommunity-poll

Abstraction: Community poll on most surprising mathematical results

Key points:

  • Math Stack Exchange community question asking for the most surprising mathematical results.
  • The Banach-Tarski Paradox is cited as the question's motivating example: a unit ball can be decomposed into finitely many disjoint parts, then reassembled via rotation and translation into two complete balls of the same radius.
  • The paradox relies on the Axiom of Choice to construct non-measurable sets; the pieces have no well-defined volume.
  • Voting system is used to surface the results most widely found surprising by the mathematical community.
