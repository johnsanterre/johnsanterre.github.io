Altera CEO Sees 'Huge Opportunity' To 'Grab Share' From AMD After Intel-Silver Lake Sale

fpgasemiconductorsalteraintelamdedge-ai

Abstraction: Altera FPGA spinoff from Intel pursues AMD Xilinx share under Silver Lake ownership

Key points:

  • Silver Lake acquired 51% of Altera from Intel for ~$3.3B (total valuation ~$8.8B); Intel retains 49%; deal valued Altera which Intel originally acquired in 2015 for $16.7B
  • CEO Raghib Hussain argues AMD's Xilinx is being neglected (talent redirected to GPU/AI) just as Altera was under Intel — creating a market-share opportunity
  • H1 2025 revenue: $816M (+16% YoY); gross margin 55%; IPO expected ~3 years out, not 2026
  • Strategy shift: expand from telco/data center focus to broader FPGA markets (industrial, aerospace-defense, test/measurement, audio-visual)
  • FPGAs positioned as complementary glue logic for GPUs and ASICs in edge AI — converting heterogeneous sensor data (LIDAR, radar, cameras) into a unified format; not GPU replacements
  • Channel/distribution investment is a priority to serve the ~10,000-customer broader FPGA market that direct sales cannot reach

Connections: Altera · Intel · Amd · Silver Lake · Fpga · Edge AI
