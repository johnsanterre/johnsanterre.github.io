25-year-old turned down Yale for a state school in 2017—now she makes $90,000 a year and has 'no regrets'

higher-educationcareeranalyticscollege-choiceivy-league

Abstraction: First-gen student chose Auburn over Yale; thriving analytically career

Key points:

  • Carolina Williams went viral in 2017 for a Papa Johns-themed Yale admissions essay; chose Auburn University instead
  • Auburn's analytics and marketing program, proximity to family in Tennessee, and larger campus size won her over
  • Graduated December 2020 with BS in business administration (analytics and marketing focus)
  • Now earns $90,000+ at Nissan's North American Future Lab as innovation and UX planner
  • Her salary exceeds the mid-career median for both Auburn ($67,500) and Yale ($89,700) graduates per Payscale
  • Cost after aid was roughly equal; Yale's prestige was not a decisive factor as first-gen student
