The future of computers is only $4 away, with Raspberry Pi CEO Eben Upton

Abstraction: Raspberry Pi origins, philosophy, chip design, and semiconductor shortage impact

Key points:

  • Raspberry Pi was founded to increase CS applicants to Cambridge University (from ~200 back toward 600); grew to 7 million units/year by 2021 despite the chip shortage limiting growth
  • Eben Upton's philosophy: general-purpose computers (vs locked-down appliances) are necessary to "beguile" people into becoming programmers, as the Commodore 64 once did
  • Raspberry Pi designed its own RP2040 microcontroller chip to vertically integrate and justify software investment; chip design requires ~5-10 distinct specialist roles and cost roughly $5M
  • The 2021-2022 semiconductor shortage left JPMC with several million units of backorder; Raspberry Pi went from "buy from stock tomorrow" to 3-4 month lead times
  • The Foundation returns surplus profit to fund STEM teacher training (runs the UK's National Centre for Computing Education) and leverages it to attract multi-year philanthropic grants
