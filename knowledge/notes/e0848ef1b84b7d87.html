Abstraction: Winter-start Appalachian Trail ultralight gear list with redundancy strategy

Key points:

  • Final base weight: 20.74 lbs for a winter start; author emphasizes "tactical redundancies" (backup headlamp, extra socks) over unnecessary extras
  • Pack: ULA Circuit chosen for comfort and capacity over Osprey Exos 55 Pro and Zpacks Arc Zip 62L
  • Sleep system: Enlightened Equipment Conundrum 0°F quilt/bag on Thermarest X-Therm NXT Max
  • Footwear rotation planned across trail sections: Hoka TenNine GTX → Hoka Speedboat GTX → TOPO models
  • Electronics: COROS APEX 2 PRO watch, Garmin inReach 66i GPS, Dark Energy Poseidon Pro waterproof battery (8.8 oz)
  • Cold-weather layering preference for Merino wool and alpaca; Norrona outerwear for Norwegian cold-weather expertise
