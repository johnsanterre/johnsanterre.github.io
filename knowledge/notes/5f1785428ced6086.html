MathJax Bookmarklet

mathjaxlatexbookmarkletmath-renderingweb-tools

Abstraction: Browser bookmarklet to render LaTeX math notation on any webpage

Key points:

  • Bookmarklet dynamically renders TeX/LaTeX, MathML, and AsciiMathML on arbitrary web pages using the MathJax library
  • LaTeX delimited by $...$ (inline) or \[...\] (display) is rendered; AsciiMathML delimited by backticks is also supported
  • A second variant replaces blurry image-based math on WordPress.com blogs and Wikipedia with crisp MathJax rendering
  • The WordPress/Wikipedia variant performs image replacement in addition to all standard bookmarklet behavior

Connections: Mathjax · Wikipedia · Latex · Math Rendering
