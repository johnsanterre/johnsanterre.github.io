Abstraction: Weekly social data project for practicing data science skills

Key points:

  • TidyTuesday is a weekly social project providing real-world datasets for learning data wrangling and visualization
  • Organized by the Data Science Learning Community; supports R, Python, and Julia workflows
  • Goal is learning — participants share code and output with #TidyTuesday hashtag on social media
  • Used in at least 30 courses by 2024, exceeding their goal of 10
  • 2025 saw 51 community pull requests for dataset curation and Python/Julia improvements
  • Participants can create visualizations, models, Quarto reports, Shiny apps, or other data-science outputs
