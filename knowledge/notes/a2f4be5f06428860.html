Abstraction: Curated topic-centric directory of high-quality public datasets

Key points:

  • Community-maintained list of free (and some paid) public datasets organized into 30+ topic categories including Biology, Climate, Economics, ML, NLP, Social Networks, and Transportation
  • Originally incubated at OMNILab, Shanghai Jiao Tong University; now part of BaiYuLan Open AI community
  • Repo auto-generated from apd-core; contributions made via structured YAML files in a separate repo
  • Biology section includes 1000 Genomes, Human Microbiome Project, TCGA, UniProt, and iNaturalist (180M+ observations)
  • Data Challenges category links to Kaggle, AIcrowd, DrivenData, Netflix Prize, and KDD Cup datasets

Connections: Github · Kaggle · Open Datasets · Data Science
