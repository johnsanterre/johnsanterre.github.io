Ridge regression - Wikipedia

statisticsmachine-learningregularizationregression

Abstraction: L2 regularization adding penalty to stabilize ill-conditioned regression

Key points:

  • Adds λI to the moment matrix X^TX in OLS, reducing condition number and mitigating multicollinearity; estimator is (X^TX + λI)^{-1}X^Ty
  • Introduced by Hoerl and Kennard in 1970 in Technometrics; named after Andrey Tikhonov who independently developed it for integral equations
  • In ML called weight decay; also known as L2 regularization, Tikhonov-Phillips regularization, constrained linear inversion
  • Bayesian interpretation: equivalent to MAP estimation with a zero-mean Gaussian prior on coefficients
  • Ridge parameter λ chosen via cross-validation or Grace Wahba's generalized cross-validation minimizer
  • SVD analysis shows λ adds to singular values in denominator, shrinking small singular value contributions
