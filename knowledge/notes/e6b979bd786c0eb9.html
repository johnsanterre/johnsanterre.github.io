  • Internal alternative called "Nest" (TypeScript-based) being built as eventual default; Vercel serves as immediate unblocking "escape valve"
  • Meta already running 10 projects on Vercel+GitHub; changes deployable in minutes
  • Meta's internal coding assistant Devmate uses Anthropic's Claude because Meta's own Llama struggled with complex tasks
  • Pattern illustrates how even hyperscalers adopt external tooling for fast-moving AI research teams
