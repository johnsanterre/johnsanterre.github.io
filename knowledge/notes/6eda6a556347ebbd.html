Abstraction: R package for antimicrobial resistance analysis and microbial taxonomy

Key points:

  • Provides taxonomically valid microorganism lookup across 96,982 records and 625 antimicrobial drugs
  • Key functions: as.mo() for arbitrary input to valid microbial taxonomy; as.ab() for antibiotic IDs; as.sir() for MIC/disk diffusion SIR interpretation
  • Supports EUCAST and CLSI interpretive rules; eucast_rules() and clsi_rules() apply breakpoints automatically
  • mdro() and related functions classify multidrug-resistant organisms (MDRO, BRMO, MRGN, MDR-TB)
  • antibiogram() / wisca() generate traditional, combination, and syndromic antibiograms with ggplot2 integration
  • ab_from_text() and av_from_text() extract drug names and doses from free-text clinical records
