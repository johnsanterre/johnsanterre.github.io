Abstraction: Recommended photography and creative documentaries for artistic inspiration

Key points:

  • Erwin Blumenfeld was the world's highest-paid photographer in the 1940s-50s, shooting Vogue and Harper's Bazaar
  • Gregory Crewdson creates cinematic single-image narratives of small-town America using film-set-scale production
  • Diana Vreeland edited Harper's Bazaar and Vogue from the 1930s to 1970s; creative inspiration beyond photography
  • Slim Aarons documented luxury lifestyle; his 1970s work is widely imitated by today's Instagram travel photographers
  • Reine Paradis shoots fine-art photography using real locations rather than composite compositing
