An e-commerce CEO is getting absolutely roasted online for laying off 90% of his support staff after an AI chatbot outperformed them

ai-automationjob-displacementcustomer-supportchatbotstartup

Abstraction: Dukaan CEO replaces 23 of 26 support staff with AI chatbot, sparks backlash

Key points:

  • Suumit Shah, CEO of Dukaan (Bengaluru e-commerce platform), laid off 23 of 26 customer support staff in September 2022
  • AI chatbot responded in under 2 minutes vs human staff average of 2+ hours; reduced support costs ~85% (monthly budget now $100)
  • Shah's Twitter thread announcing layoffs went viral with 1.5M+ views and was widely criticized (600+ critical quote-tweets)
  • Critics argued real cause was business failure and dry funding, not AI performance
  • Contrasted with NEDA helpline that replaced entire staff with chatbot, later disabled for giving harmful information on eating disorders
  • Shah maintained "AI is taking our jobs" and everyone will eventually do this
