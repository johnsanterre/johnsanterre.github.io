reinforcement-learninghuman-computer-interactionworkshoppersonalization

Abstraction: Workshop agenda building RL research agenda for human-computer interaction

Key points:

  • RL4HCI was a 1-day online workshop co-located with ACM CHI 2021 (May 8), organized by Andrew Howes at Aalto University
  • Goal: build a research community at the intersection of reinforcement learning and HCI, integrating user modeling with interactive RL systems
  • Interactive RL systems must model user visual search, multitasking, decision-making, emotions, and social connectivity to be genuinely adaptive
  • Target application domains highlighted: personalization, tutoring systems, medical diagnosis, and interaction design
  • Accepted 2-3 page position papers; PhD students encouraged to submit plans for working at the RL-HCI intersection

Connections: Reinforcement Learning · Human Computer Interaction · AI Agents
