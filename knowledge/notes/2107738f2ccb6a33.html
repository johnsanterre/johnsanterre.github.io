The Generative AI Copyright Fight Is Just Getting Started

generative-aicopyrightfair-usetraining-datalaw

Abstraction: Legal debate over AI training on copyrighted works and fair use

Key points:

  • Authors Guild CEO Mary Rasenberger: AI training is not fair use; guild sued OpenAI and Microsoft; average full-time author earns ~$20,000/year vs OpenAI's ~$90B valuation
  • AI builders argue training extracts statistical signals, not reproductions, qualifying as fair use — analogous to search indexing
  • Lawyer Matthew Butterick framed the fight as tech companies accumulating power over copyright holders
  • Mike Masnick (Techdirt): requiring AI companies to pay creators would entrench large players and shut out smaller competitors
  • Authors Guild building a licensing tool so generative AI companies can pay to use member works
  • GitHub Copilot and image generators like Midjourney can closely mimic works from their training sets, strengthening creators' harm claims

Connections: Openai · Authors Guild · Generative AI · Copyright · Fair Use
