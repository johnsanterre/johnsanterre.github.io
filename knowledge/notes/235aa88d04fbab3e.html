Abstraction: Voice AI platform for noise cancellation and transcription

Key points:

  • Three separate products: AI Meeting Assistant (bot-free note taker), Call Center AI (with Accent Conversion and Agent Assist), and AI Voice SDK for developers.
  • All products share the same underlying AI voice engine but are installed individually.
  • Works at the audio level so it integrates with any conferencing app: Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack Huddles.
  • Supports on-device English transcription for privacy plus server-based transcription in 15 additional languages.
  • Custom Vocabulary supports up to 750 words for domain-specific terms and acronyms.
  • Mobile app supports offline recording and transcription; noise cancellation on mobile is forthcoming.
