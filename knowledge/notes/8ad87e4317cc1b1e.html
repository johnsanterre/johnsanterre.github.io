Abstraction: Large-scale image database organized by WordNet hierarchy for CV research

Key points:

  • ImageNet organizes images according to the WordNet noun hierarchy, with each node depicted by hundreds to thousands of images
  • Instrumental in advancing computer vision and deep learning research
  • Data available free for non-commercial research use
  • The project changed the direction of AI research, particularly enabling breakthroughs in deep convolutional networks

Connections: Imagenet · Wordnet · Computer Vision · Deep Learning · Image Classification
