Elon Musk Has Fired Twitter's 'Ethical AI' Team

ai-ethicstwitteralgorithmic-biastech-layoffsresponsible-ai

Abstraction: Elon Musk eliminates Twitter's ML Ethics, Transparency, and Accountability team

Key points:

  • Elon Musk's post-acquisition Twitter layoffs eliminated the entire ML Ethics, Transparency, and Accountability (META) team led by Rumman Chowdhury
  • The META team had published research on political bias (finding right-leaning sources were amplified more than left-leaning), disclosed photo-cropping algorithm racial bias, and ran one of the first "bias bounty" contests for outside researchers
  • Team had been told "not to rock the boat" and research was put on hold during the acquisition period
  • Outside experts called it one of the only credible corporate AI ethics groups that engaged substantively with academics and the public
  • Musk's stated goal of open-sourcing "the algorithm" was complicated by the fact that many different algorithms drive Twitter's feed, not one; understanding them requires real-time data
