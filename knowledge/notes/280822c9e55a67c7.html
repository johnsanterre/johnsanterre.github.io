Speechify: Text to Speech & Voice Typing AI Assistant | 60M+ Users

text-to-speechvoice-aiaccessibilityproductivity

Abstraction: AI voice productivity assistant for TTS, dictation, and research

Key points:

  • Speechify is an all-in-one Voice AI productivity platform combining text-to-speech, voice typing/dictation, AI note-taking, AI podcast creation, and conversational research — targeting users who prefer voice over typing
  • Supports PDF, EPUB, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, web links, and scanned pages; AI voices are described as indistinguishable from human voices and available in multiple languages
  • Offers voice cloning from a few seconds of audio (with speaker permission) for personalized listening
  • Provides a text-to-speech API with instant voice cloning, streaming, SSML, emotional control, and speech marks; powers tens of millions of users
  • Positioned against ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity as a voice-first alternative; marketed for accessibility (dyslexia, students) and productivity (speed listening at 2x-3x)
