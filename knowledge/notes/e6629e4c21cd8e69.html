The Smartest Bet In Silicon Valley

silicon-valleystartup-culturesvbventure-capitalfounders

Abstraction: Foundation Capital VC defends startup founders after Silicon Valley Bank collapse

Key points:

  • Written by Steve Vassallo of Foundation Capital in April 2023 following Silicon Valley Bank's collapse
  • Argues founders bore the brunt of SVB's failure — many moved company bills to personal credit cards and transferred personal savings to make payroll before regulators guaranteed all deposits
  • Criticizes media characterization of founders as entitled "tech bros," distinguishing them from billionaire VCs and celebrity executives
  • Eight Fed rate increases raised cost of capital ~20x; S&P and crypto indices fell; IPO window frozen; late-stage private valuations down a third
  • Invokes Silicon Valley's garage-origin mythology: Brin/Page at Menlo Park, Jobs/Wozniak at Los Altos, Gordon Moore at Palo Alto
  • Core thesis: "Bet on founders" — the attempt to build is the spirit of Silicon Valley, not the outcomes

Connections: Silicon Valley Bank · Foundation Capital · Startup Culture · Venture Capital
