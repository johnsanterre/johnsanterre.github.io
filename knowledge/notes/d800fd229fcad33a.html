Accenture CTO says 'there will be some consolidation' of jobs but 'the biggest worry is of the jobs for people who won't be using generative AI' | Fortune

Abstraction: Accenture CTO warns AI non-adopters face greater job risk than adopters

Key points:

  • Paul Daugherty (Accenture CTO) argues businesses must invest more in developing their existing workforce for AI than in the technology itself
  • "There is no AI-ready workforce you can hire" — companies must train current employees, not wait to recruit pre-trained talent
  • AI will cause some job "consolidation" (fewer people for same tasks) but the bigger risk is workers who never adopt generative AI
  • Effective AI use requires good prompt engineering and keeping humans in the loop to add judgment that models lack
  • Critical thinking, reasoning, and problem-solving flagged at Fortune Brainstorm AI 2023 as key skills in the AI age
