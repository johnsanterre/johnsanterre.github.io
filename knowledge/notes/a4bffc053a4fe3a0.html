AI Virtues As Missing Bedrock Ingredient For Responsible AI Says AI Ethics And AI Law

ai-ethicsresponsible-aivirtue-ethicsai-lawfairness

Abstraction: Virtue ethics framework proposed as foundation for AI ethics principles

Key points:

  • Researcher Thilo Hagendorff (Philosophy & Technology, 2022) proposes four basic AI Virtues derived by clustering existing AI ethics principles: Justice, Honesty, Responsibility, and Care
  • Two second-order AI Virtues are also proposed: Prudence (counteracting individual psychological biases) and Fortitude (counteracting situational/organizational forces)
  • AI Virtue of Justice maps to algorithmic fairness, non-discrimination, bias mitigation; Honesty maps to transparency, explainability, interpretability; Responsibility to accountability and accuracy; Care to non-maleficence, privacy, safety, and beneficence
  • Argument is that virtues are the bedrock soil from which ethical rules (the flowers) naturally grow — without getting virtues right first, endless rule-making fails
  • Criticism: risk of distraction from existing AI ethics work; agent-centric virtues vs. act-centric principles tension; some resist adding more frameworks to an already crowded space

Connections: AI Ethics · Responsible AI · AI Safety
