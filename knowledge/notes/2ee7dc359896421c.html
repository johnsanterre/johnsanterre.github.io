Glasgow Willy Wonka experience called a 'farce' as tickets refunded

event-fraudai-generated-contentconsumer-deceptionviral-news

Abstraction: AI-generated promotional imagery used to fraudulently market fake immersive event

Key points:

  • Glasgow event "Willy's Chocolate Experience" charged up to £35 per ticket promising giant mushrooms, candy canes, chocolate fountains, and Oompa-Loompas
  • Venue was a sparsely decorated warehouse with plastic props and a small bouncy castle; police were called
  • Event was cancelled midway through its opening day after angry attendees demanded refunds
  • Organiser House of Illuminati issued apology citing being "let down in many areas" at the last minute
  • Hired actor revealed he was given an impossible script with no promised props and told to improvise
  • Full refunds promised within 10 days; event became viral example of AI-generated marketing fraud
