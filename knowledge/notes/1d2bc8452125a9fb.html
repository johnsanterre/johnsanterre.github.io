Design and Code with ChatGPT and Midjourney

courseapp-developmentswiftuireactai-designtutorial

Abstraction: Course building apps with GPT-4 and Midjourney

Key points:

  • Design+Code course (~4 hrs, by Meng To) teaching beginners to design and build apps using OpenAI's GPT-4 and Midjourney with prompts for SwiftUI, React, CSS, app concepts, icons, and copywriting.
  • 15 video lessons: GPT-4 for building apps, Midjourney for product design, then hands-on SwiftUI builds (sunflower pattern, animate galaxy, card design/drag gesture/particles) and React+CSS work (shiny 3D card, dark mode, mouse-follow radial gradient).
  • Uses ChatGPT for code generation, error handling (via CodeSandbox), and "self-reflection" prompting; Midjourney for AI-generated visuals, icons, and image-to-vector.
  • Part of a broader DesignCode catalog (50+ courses) that increasingly features Cursor, Claude AI, Claude Artifacts, v0.dev, and Ideogram for AI-driven app building.

Connections: Chatgpt · Midjourney · GPT-4 · AI Assisted Coding · Swiftui
