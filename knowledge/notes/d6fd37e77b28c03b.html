Why Tech Job Interviews Became Such a Nightmare

tech-hiringsoftware-engineeringinterviewslayoffs

Abstraction: Tech hiring shift toward grueling interviews amid post-pandemic layoffs

Key points:

  • Over 400,000 tech jobs shed since start of 2022; tech sector unemployment at 2.3% per CompTIA vs. 3.7% overall US
  • Meta specifically flagged for down-leveling candidates and offering salaries up to $50,000 below comparable roles at other Big Tech firms
  • Interviewing.io analyzed 20 Meta offers and found stark pattern of lowball offers exploiting lack of competing bids
  • CoderPad CEO recommends capping take-home tests at 90 minutes to 2 hours; long tests bias against candidates with caregiving responsibilities
  • Balance of power has shifted to employers, causing overcorrection from pandemic-era over-hiring
  • Collaborative live-coding problems recommended over solo take-home builds as better predictors of actual work fit

Connections: Meta · Interviewing IO · Coderpad · Tech Hiring · Software Engineering Interviews
