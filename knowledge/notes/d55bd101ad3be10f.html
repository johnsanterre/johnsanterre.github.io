AI Drew This Gorgeous Comics Series. You'd Never Know It

ai-artmidjourneycomicsgenerative-arttext-to-image

Abstraction: Midjourney AI used to create full professional comics series

Key points:

  • Campfire Entertainment's The Bestiary Chronicles is believed to be the first AI-art comics series; all four parts are free for download and also sold in print anthologies.
  • Creator Steve Coulson used Midjourney by iterating text prompts (e.g., "Hitchcock Blonde," style references like J.C. Leyendecker) and selecting the best outputs — likened to improv jazz collaboration.
  • Midjourney released two upgrades during the three-month production window, producing a visible jump in image quality between issues.
  • Coulson gave Midjourney an author credit and describes the workflow as "collage": generating 100+ splash pages, then assembling narrative around the best selections.
  • Raises ethical and copyright concerns: AI art trains on human-made work, and an AI image winning an art prize in September 2022 sparked artist backlash ("death of artistry").
