How To Delete Your Data From ChatGPT

privacydata-deletionchatgptgdprai-regulation

Abstraction: Guide to ChatGPT data deletion rights and privacy controls amid regulatory scrutiny

Key points:

  • ChatGPT trains on web data, licensed data, and user chat inputs by default; OpenAI is secretive about exact training corpus composition
  • Italy temporarily banned ChatGPT over GDPR; the European EDPB formed a task force on ChatGPT; Canada also launched an investigation
  • OpenAI's Personal Data Removal Request form (primarily for Europe and Japan) targets removing mentions from model outputs, not from training data
  • Toggling off "Chat History & Training" in Settings stops conversations from being used in future training; data retained 30 days for abuse monitoring then deleted
  • OpenAI acknowledges it is "technically impossible" to correct factual inaccuracies in model outputs at present
  • ChatGPT has generated false defamatory statements about real named individuals, including a law professor and an Australian mayor
