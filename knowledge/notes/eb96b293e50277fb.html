Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces

operating-systemstextbookfreevirtualizationconcurrency

Abstraction: Free OS textbook covering virtualization, concurrency, and persistence

Key points:

  • Written by Remzi H. Arpaci-Dusseau and Andrea C. Arpaci-Dusseau (UW-Madison); permanently free in PDF form, version 1.10 as of November 2023
  • Three core themes: virtualization (CPU scheduling, memory management), concurrency (threads, locks), and persistence (file systems, storage)
  • Available in multiple formats: free PDF, Lulu hardcover ($39.75), Amazon softcover ($28.27), paid enhanced PDF ($10.00)
  • Known as "the comet book" or "the asteroid book" among students; includes homework problems and projects
  • BibTeX citation provided; authors encourage citations to support the free-textbook model
