Abstraction: Comprehensive freely available computer vision textbook by Richard Szeliski

Key points:

  • PDF freely available for personal use at szeliski.org/Book; electronic version has different pagination from print
  • Based on UW CSE 576 courses co-taught with Steve Seitz (2001, 2005, 2008, 2020) and Stanford 2003 with David Fleet
  • First edition published by Springer; available in Chinese and Japanese translations
  • Second edition posted as drafts for reader feedback (errata, suggestions via email)
  • PDF enabled for commenting and hyperlinks to sections, equations, and references
  • Last updated March 2020 at time of snapshot
