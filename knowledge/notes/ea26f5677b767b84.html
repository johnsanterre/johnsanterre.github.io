Abstraction: Microsoft-OpenAI partnership fracturing over competing AI ambitions

Key points:

  • Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff says Microsoft's hiring of Mustafa Suleyman (DeepMind/Inflection co-founder) as Microsoft AI CEO cracked the Microsoft-OpenAI relationship, as Suleyman and Sam Altman are known rivals
  • OpenAI joined SoftBank and Oracle on the $500B Stargate data center project, ending Microsoft's exclusive cloud provider status
  • Microsoft invested $1B in OpenAI in 2019; their exclusive agreement was set to end when AGI is achieved (with a financial definition agreed between both parties)
  • Microsoft is reportedly building its own frontier LLM called MAI-1 and launched a new AI group led by Jay Parikh without mentioning OpenAI
  • Tensions date to at least mid-2023 after OpenAI launched its own enterprise product competing with Microsoft
  • Salesforce, also an Anthropic investor, benefits from OpenAI broadening its cloud partnerships
