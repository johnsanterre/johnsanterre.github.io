Leaked org chart reveals the 58 top leaders and engineers at Google DeepMind

google-deepmindorg-structureai-competitionleadership

Abstraction: Google DeepMind org structure after 2023 Brain-DeepMind merger

Key points:

  • Google merged Google Brain with DeepMind in April 2023 to counter Microsoft/OpenAI threat
  • Demis Hassabis (DeepMind co-founder) leads the combined unit of ~2,450 FTEs
  • Jeff Dean moved from management to Chief Scientist reporting directly to CEO Sundar Pichai; now working on Gemini
  • Eli Collins (overseeing Bard chatbot) now reports to Hassabis under the new structure
  • Zoubin Ghahramani (~600 employees) shifted from reporting to Jeff Dean to Koray Kavukcuoglu (VP Research)
  • A dedicated Path to Artificial General Intelligence (PAGI) group exists within the organization

Connections: Google Deepmind · Google · Openai · Demis Hassabis · Large Language Models · Artificial General Intelligence
