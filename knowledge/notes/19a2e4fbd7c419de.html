Understanding Wavelets

waveletssignal-processingmathematicsfourier-transformdsp

Abstraction: Wavelet transform as a signal analysis tool preserving time-frequency locality

Key points:

  • Fourier transform decomposes signals into infinite sine waves but loses time-domain information; the wavelet transform addresses this limitation.
  • Wavelet decomposition uses finite functions (wavelets) that meet specific mathematical conditions, rather than infinite sinusoids.
  • Artem Kirsanov describes the wavelet transform as a "mathematical microscope" — it can zoom in on both time and frequency simultaneously.
  • Conceptually similar to windowed Fourier analysis but more flexible; the choice of wavelet basis affects the time-frequency resolution tradeoff.
  • Accessible introductory video requires only basic math familiarity and makes few assumptions about prior DSP knowledge.
