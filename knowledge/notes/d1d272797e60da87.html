Abstraction: TED AI 2023 first AI-only TED conference debates AGI benefits and risks

Key points:

  • TED AI 2023 (San Francisco, October 2023) was TED's first conference devoted solely to AI, with 30+ talks organized into four tracks: Intelligence & Scale, Synthetics & Realities, Autonomy & Dependence, Art & Storytelling
  • Andrew Ng framed AI as "the new electricity" and argued it poses no cause for fear; Max Tegmark countered that governments allowed AI to advance without adequate regulation
  • ChatGPT's release 10 months prior was broadly credited as the reason for the conference's existence; it caught even veteran AI researchers off guard
  • OpenAI Chief Scientist Ilya Sutskever was the sole OpenAI speaker and commanded rapt attention at the historic Herbst Theater
  • Joon Sung Park presented the Smallville 25-agent simulation and Yohei Nakajima presented BabyAGI as examples of current agentic AI applications
  • No consensus emerged on whether AGI's near-term arrival would be beneficial or dangerous
