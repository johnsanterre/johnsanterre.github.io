Sergey Brin found Gemini on Google's internal list of banned coding tools

ai-adoptionengineering-culturegoogle

Abstraction: Brin fought Google's own rule barring engineers from coding with Gemini

Key points:

  • On the All-In podcast Brin said Gemini sat on an internal 'no list' of tools engineers could not use for coding.
  • Nobody enforced it, but the page existed; he spent weeks fighting it and finally asked Sundar Pichai to fix it.
  • Brin called a junior employee being able to tell the founder no 'a sign of a healthy culture'.
  • Example of internal policy lagging a company's own AI strategy.
