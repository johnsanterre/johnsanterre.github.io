How to build your company's engineering brand

engineering-culturehiringdeveloper-relationscontent-strategy

Abstraction: Playbook for building engineering brand to accelerate technical hiring

Key points:

  • Engineering brand is better framed as investing in the engineering experience of the existing team rather than pure external PR — this builds momentum and avoids extracting from team energy
  • Core playbook: create content (blog posts, conference talks, podcasts, open-source), experiment with distribution channels, and have a single resourced owner (eventually a half-time role)
  • Direct distribution (sending content to candidates during recruiting) is more impactful than social media reach, which requires an existing network to work well
  • Avoid open-source purely for brand purposes — it has high ongoing maintenance overhead; focus on fewer, larger projects
  • Key metrics: distinct engineers contributing, healthy distribution channels, % of applicants citing eng brand content, % inbound candidates
  • Quality bar and author diversity are common friction points; allow any approver (not unanimous) and publish casual content in separate sections to avoid deterring repeat contributors
