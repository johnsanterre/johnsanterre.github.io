Abstraction: Compact four-page linear algebra reference covering vectors through eigenvalues

Key points:

  • Covers vector operations, matrix operations, linear transformations, fundamental vector spaces, and matrix representation
  • Includes solving systems of linear equations via RREF (reduced row echelon form) and computing matrix inverse
  • Covers eigenvalues and eigenvectors; targets students with upcoming exams on Ax=b problems
  • Complementary resource: "A Geometric Review of Linear Algebra" by Eero P. Simoncelli (NYU) for visual learners
  • Also links to Stanford cs229 linear algebra review and a Boulder engineering supplement for additional perspectives
