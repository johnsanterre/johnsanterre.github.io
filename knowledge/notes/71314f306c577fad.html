6 people who made up to $500,000 a year secretly working multiple remote jobs

overemploymentremote-worktech-industrylabor

Abstraction: Tech workers secretly holding multiple remote jobs earning up to $500k

Key points:

  • Six workers earned $90k-$500k+ annually by secretly holding 2-3 full-time remote jobs simultaneously, primarily in IT/network engineering
  • Workers justify it as layoff protection: one worker had two of three jobs eliminated in layoffs, validating the strategy
  • Remote job postings on LinkedIn fell from 20%+ (April 2022) to ~10% (Dec 2023), yet remote roles still drew 46% of all applications
  • ~263,000 tech workers laid off in 2023 (Amazon, Meta, Google); ~50,000 more in early 2024, fueling overemployment as self-insurance
  • Common ethical defense: CEOs routinely hold multiple executive/board roles; outcome-based pay means completing work matters, not hours clocked
  • Legal risk: no labor law violation if no contract clause prohibits it, but termination risk if discovered

Connections: Remote Work · Overemployment
