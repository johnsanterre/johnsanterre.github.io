Key points:

  • Absci CEO Sean McClain claimed at J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference 2023 to be "first and only company to design and validate new antibodies with zero-shot generative AI"
  • McClain stated the approach "completely eliminates all the biological discovery technologies that currently exist"
  • STAT News investigation suggests the documentary evidence from Absci and Generate Biomedicines does not fully support their bold AI-from-scratch drug design claims
  • The article reflects broader concerns about AI hype in biotech coinciding with ChatGPT's early popularity in early 2023
  • Absci was founded in 2011 and had a market cap near $2B at time of the presentation
