Mozilla Weighs in on Accountability Legislation - PATA can help keep the Internet in the public's best interest

platform-accountabilitytech-regulationtransparencydisinformationinternet-policy

Abstraction: Mozilla endorsing bipartisan PATA bill for platform researcher data access

Key points:

  • Platform Accountability and Transparency Act (PATA) is bipartisan, co-sponsored by Sens. Coons, Cassidy, Klobuchar, Cornyn, Blumenthal, and Romney
  • PATA would require social media platforms to provide data access for public-interest research projects
  • Bill creates a legal safe harbor enabling legitimate public-interest research free from threats of legal action
  • Also establishes ad transparency requirements for major platforms
  • Mozilla has a track record of advocating for researcher data access in the US and globally (EU DSA, Twitter API access, etc.)
  • Mozilla Head of US Public Policy Jenn Hodges: transparency is "a critical step to understanding and effectively solving hidden harms online"
