Samsung workers made a major error by using ChatGPT

data-privacychatgptenterprise-aisamsungtrade-secrets

Abstraction: Samsung engineers leaked trade secrets via ChatGPT input data retention

Key points:

  • Three separate incidents within one month of Samsung engineers leaking confidential data via ChatGPT prompts
  • Leaked data included: semiconductor chip test sequence source code, internal meeting notes converted to presentations
  • ChatGPT retains user input to train itself, meaning Samsung trade secrets are now effectively in OpenAI's possession
  • Samsung Electronics issued internal warning; Samsung Semiconductor began building its own in-house AI limited to 1,024-byte prompts
  • No recourse exists to retrieve or delete the data from OpenAI's servers
  • GDPR non-compliance concerns: Italy banned ChatGPT nationwide partly for the same reason (inability to request data deletion)

Connections: Samsung · Openai · Chatgpt · Data Privacy · Enterprise AI Risk
