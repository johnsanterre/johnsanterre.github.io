Leica Hopes Its New $9,500 Camera Can Save Photojournalism From AI

watermarkingdeepfakesphotojournalismcontent-authenticityai-detection

Abstraction: Leica M11-P camera embeds Content Credentials to authenticate photojournalism images

Key points:

  • The Leica M11-P ($9,500) is designed for professional photojournalists and embeds cryptographic Content Credentials into image metadata at capture time
  • Content Credentials use cryptographic asset hashing per the Content Authenticity Initiative (CAI) standard, an industry group of media companies, tech platforms, and NGOs
  • If an image is later altered (e.g., via Photoshop), the edit history is also recorded in the metadata
  • Credentials can be verified through an online CAI portal
  • Motivation is combating AI-generated deepfakes and digitally altered images flooding the internet
  • Watermarking was one of the commitments made by top AI companies in the Biden administration AI safety meeting
