Key points:

  • Book has two parts: (1) overview of ML interview process and role types; (2) 200+ knowledge questions with difficulty ratings

  • Covers what interviewers look for and how to signal the right skills for different ML roles
  • Companion course CS 329S at Stanford covers ML systems design questions
  • Includes 30 open-ended ML systems design questions that nearly all companies ask and candidates find hardest
  • Source code available on GitHub; Discord community for discussing answers
