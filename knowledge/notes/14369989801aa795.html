How to Build a Small Solar Power System

solar-poweroff-griddiylow-voltagedc-power

Abstraction: DIY small-scale off-grid solar PV system guide

Key points:

  • Omitting an inverter and running low-voltage DC directly avoids 50% conversion losses that occur in small-scale AC/DC setups
  • Batteries account for 80–90% of total costs and energy invested in off-grid solar systems; direct-drive batteryless systems are often preferable
  • Lead-acid batteries should not discharge below 50% capacity; lithium-ion limit is 15%
  • 12V solar panels actually output ~20V in full sun; a DC-DC buck converter is needed to deliver a stable lower voltage to devices
  • Cheap solar charge controllers work fine for small systems; MPPT controllers are not worth the cost at small scale
  • Series wiring of panels doubles voltage while parallel wiring doubles current; always fuse close to the power source
