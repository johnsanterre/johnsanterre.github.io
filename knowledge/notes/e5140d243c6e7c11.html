SendCutSend — online laser cutting and sheet-metal fabrication

on-demand-manufacturinglaser-cuttingsheet-metalcncfabrication

Abstraction: Upload-a-file on-demand laser cutting service

Key points:

  • On-demand digital fabrication service: upload a design file, get laser-cut sheet-metal/parts shipped — the "upload → instant quote → parts in days" model for custom hardware.
  • Services beyond cutting: multiaxis CNC machining in billet, bending (within 1°), dimple forming (up to 3"), hardware insertion (PEM-style fasteners), tapping/threading.
  • Finishing options: Class II anodizing (5 colors), zinc/nickel plating, powder coat (11 options), deburring, surface graining.
  • Representative of the consumer-accessible end of on-demand manufacturing — hobbyist to production runs without owning machines.
