Yann LeCun

Person. Pioneer of convolutional neural networks (LeNet, 1989 — reading ZIP codes at Bell Labs) and longtime Chief AI Scientist at Meta. Turing Award (2018, with Hinton & Bengio).

The field's loudest contrarian on LLM scaling: argues autoregressive LLMs are an off-ramp on the road to real intelligence, dismisses near-term existential risk, and pushes alternative architectures — world models, JEPA, energy-based methods — predicting a "new paradigm of AI architectures" within 5 years (a claim captured in this corpus). Useful as the counterweight node to the safety cluster.
