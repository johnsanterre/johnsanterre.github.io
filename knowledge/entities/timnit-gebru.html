Timnit Gebru

Person. AI ethics researcher who anchors the accountability wing of the field. Co-authored Gender Shades (with Joy Buolamwini — facial recognition's race/gender error gaps) and "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots," the LLM-critique paper whose fallout led to her high-profile ouster from Google's Ethical AI team (2020). Founded DAIR (Distributed AI Research Institute) to do community-rooted AI research outside Big Tech.
