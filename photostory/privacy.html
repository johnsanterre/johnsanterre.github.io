PhotoStory privacy policy

Effective 19 September 2026. PhotoStory is a private family photo album run by John Santerre.

What PhotoStory is

PhotoStory is a web application where family members and invited friends upload photographs, record spoken stories about them, and share albums with one another. It is not a public service and it carries no advertising.

What we collect

When you create an account with a username and password we store your name, email address, username and a hashed password. When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address from Google and nothing else; we do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Google Drive, Google Photos or contacts. We store the photographs, audio recordings, notes and captions you add, and a log of sign-ins and actions (time, IP address, browser) used to keep the service secure.

How we use it

Your information is used only to run the album: to show you and the people you share with the photographs and stories, to send you account emails such as verification and password reset, and to protect the service against abuse. Audio recordings may be transcribed and summarised by an automated service so that stories can be read as well as heard.

Who can see it

Photographs and recordings are visible only to the people an album has been shared with, or to anyone holding a link if an album's owner has made it public. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. The service runs on Google Cloud, and audio transcription and summaries use OpenAI's API; those providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

Your control

You can edit or delete your photographs, recordings and notes at any time, and you can delete your whole account from the Settings page, which removes your data. Questions or deletion requests can also be sent to john.santerre.ai@gmail.com.
