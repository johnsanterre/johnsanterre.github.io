About

I hold a PhD in artificial intelligence from a computer science department, and I've spent my career at the seam between research, teaching, and industry — graduate machine learning at UC Berkeley and SMU, and a standing obsession with the mathematical structure underneath it all. I actively advise companies on AI — I've served as CTO and Chief AI Officer, and as technical advisor to startups from first pitch through scale — work with me.

That advising rests on operating roles: founder and managing director of Silicon Valley Bank's AI Lab, chief data scientist at a venture-backed health startup, special project lead at DeepLearning.AI, and research years at SAP and Argonne National Laboratory. Today I also advise a NASA Goddard planetary-science team on computer vision for the exploration of Europa and Titan.

The through-line in everything here is legibility: a field this important shouldn't require a decoder ring. Courses, the mind map, workbooks, talks — they're all the same project in different forms.

Before the academy I was a photojournalist — Haiti, Thailand, Cambodia, and elsewhere. That work continues as scrocco, and it turns out to be the same discipline: choosing what to leave out.
