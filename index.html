About

I hold a PhD in artificial intelligence from a computer science department, and I've spent my career at the seam between research and teaching — graduate machine learning at UC Berkeley and SMU, advisory work in industry, and a standing obsession with the mathematical structure underneath it all.

The through-line in everything here is legibility: a field this important shouldn't require a decoder ring. Courses, the mind map, workbooks, talks — they're all the same project in different forms.

Before the academy I was a photojournalist — Haiti, Thailand, Cambodia, and elsewhere. That work continues as scrocco, and it turns out to be the same discipline: choosing what to leave out.
