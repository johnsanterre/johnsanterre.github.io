Fast Last-Iterate Convergence of SGD in the Smooth Interpolation Regime

We study population convergence guarantees of stochastic gradient descent (SGD) for smooth convex objectives in the interpolation regime, where the noise at optimum is zero or near zero. The behavior of the last iterate of SGD in this setting
